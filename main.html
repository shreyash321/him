What is Kalyan Matka ?

Kalyan Matka is a popular and common number-based game that originally arise in the city of Mumbai, Maharashtra India. The game was originated by a man named Kalyanji Bhagat. Kalyan Matka game is usually played by betting on certain numbers. Each player selects a specific number and a number is announced at the designated time. If the selected number matches the declared number, then that player gets the prize as winner.

The main masterpiece of this game is its simple rules and attractive reward system. There are various types of betting methods to play kalyan matka, such as single, jodi, panel etc. It has become easier and more popular in today's generation by playing matka online. However, since it is a gambling game, it should be played with attention and be aware of its legal aspects.

History Of Online Matka ?

In the original version of the game, bets were focused on the opening and closing rates of cotton, which were sent to the Bombay Cotton Exchange from the New York Cotton Exchange via teleprinters. When the exchange stopped functioning in 1961 people started seeking new ways to wager bets. Ratan Khatri came up with an idea of using playing cards to declare opening and closing rates. Numbers were written on slips of paper and placed in a matka, a large earthen pot. One person would then draw a slip and announce the winning numbers. Eventually, the practice shifted to drawing three numbers from a pack of playing cards, but the name "matka" persisted.
